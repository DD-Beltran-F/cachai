We've published our first article about cachai!

11 August 2025

We are very happy to announce that Cachai has its first published article! It is now available in the Research Notes of the American Astronomical Society (RNAAS) as an open-access publication:

“CACHAI’s First Module: A Fully Customizable Chord Diagram for Astronomy and Beyond”

The article, by Diego Beltrán and Dr. Maria Luiza L. Dantas, introduces the Chord Diagram module that started Cachai. We describe the motivation behind its development, how the module works, its main features, and the different ways it can be customized. We also discuss how chord diagrams can complement more traditional visualizations such as heatmaps and corner plots, particularly when we want to present many relationships in a compact and visually intuitive way.

Read the full article on IOPscience (DOI 10.3847/2515-5172/adf8df)

This is our first publication about Cachai, and hopefully the first of many!
